# D.A.K Travel theme implementation

This repository contains the custom WordPress theme for D.A.K Travel.

## Homepage direction

Primary positioning: **Complex travel, handled properly.**

The homepage prioritises:

- Groups and delegations
- Business and organisational travel
- South Africa–Israel travel
- Complex personal and international journeys

The hero visual uses a bundled consultation/coastline illustration as a safe fallback. A real approved D.A.K photograph can replace it in **Appearance → Customize → D.A.K Site Images & Logos → Homepage specialist image** without editing code.

## Deployment

Deploy and test on staging first. Keep automatic push-to-deploy disabled until the theme has been verified on the WordPress installation.

## Required pre-launch checks

- Confirm the public email address and office details.
- Confirm IATA / ASATA / Club Travel credentials and approved logo use.
- Test all enquiry forms and outbound email delivery.
- Test WhatsApp, phone and navigation links on mobile.
- Confirm privacy and booking terms.
- Verify no passenger data or booking references appear in public content.
- Confirm redirects before replacing the existing site.
